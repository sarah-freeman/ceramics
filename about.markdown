Sarah Freeman is a ceramicist based in Edmonton, Alberta. She started making pottery in 2023, when a friend encouraged her to join a lesson at Viva Clayworks. She has been working out of Viva ever since!

Her practice includes handbuilding techniques such as nerikomi and slab building, in addition to wheel-thrown works and large sculptural pieces. Recently, she has also been working on wood-fired pieces and mixing her own glazes. Her work is inspired by the calm, but never boring, peace of Edmonton's River Valley. Drawn to variegated tones and textures, she often mixes unglazed clay with organic, floating glazes. She is especially fond of blue and green tones in her work.
